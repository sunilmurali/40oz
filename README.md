## Bolt Drop Game

An exciting electromagnetic bolt-dropping game! 

### How to Play:
1. Open `game.html` in your browser
2. Hold the "HOLD TO SLIDE" button - the electromagnet will slide along the rope
3. Release the button at the perfect moment to drop the bolt
4. Try to land the bolt in the moving bucket below!
5. Score points for successful hits

### Game Features:
- **Electromagnetic Physics**: Realistic bolt drop simulation with gravity
- **Dynamic Targets**: Bucket repositions after each attempt for endless replayability
- **Score Tracking**: Keep track of your score, hits, and total attempts
- **Visual Feedback**: Particles and messages for hits and misses
- **Touch Support**: Works on both desktop and mobile devices
